# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-23

## Context

The project will make a series of technology choices (IaC tool, streaming
service, storage layout, auth) that are worth explaining to a reader who was
not there, including the future me preparing for interviews.

## Decision

Keep short Architecture Decision Records in `docs/adr/`, numbered
sequentially, one decision per file, using the sections Context, Decision,
Consequences. A superseded ADR is not edited; a new one references it.

## Consequences

Small writing overhead per decision. In exchange, the "why" behind the
architecture is on record and reviewable.
